# iPhone Safari release check

Use a physical iPhone in portrait mode. Start at [mitvahchaos.netlify.app](https://mitvahchaos.netlify.app/) in Safari. Keep the phone at its normal text size and turn off Safari's desktop-site mode.

1. Open **Play → Level 1**. Confirm the full board fits with **VIEW** off, and the mission text sits above the board. Tap a game object, then **MISSION** and **STATUS**; the board should not shift or hide the bottom controls.
2. On a compact phone, tap **VIEW** to enlarge the board. Use **↑ VIEW** and **↓ VIEW**, select the magnet bot, tap **MAGNETIC**, and apply it to the hero. Every button should respond to one tap; no floating site badge should cover a sticker or control.
3. Complete Level 1: collect three coins, deposit them in Rivky's pushka, collect the present, and reach the exit. Check that the result screen appears and Level 2 unlocks. Reload Safari and check that progress remains.
4. Test **Pause → Settings** for music and sound controls. Resume, then switch apps and return; gameplay should remain stable. Try both joystick and tap-to-move controls.
5. Add the game to the Home Screen from Safari. Open it once online, close it, put the phone in Airplane Mode, then reopen it and launch Level 1. Turn Airplane Mode off when done.

Record the iPhone model, iOS version, Safari or Home Screen mode, and any failed step. A screenshot or short screen recording of a blocked tap is especially useful.
